# Mechanical Integration

CBR-1 follows the same reparability-first mechanical philosophy as CBI Robotics' R-BOT platform: mounting points use standard hardware and easily replaceable components rather than proprietary brackets.

## Load Ratings

CBR-1 has been load-tested on two surface types:

| Surface | Payload range tested |
|---|---|
| Flat floor | 50–100 kg |
| All-terrain | 50–100 kg |

Do not exceed the tested payload range for your surface without consulting [support@cbirobotics.com](mailto:support@cbirobotics.com).

## Mounting

- Use the top deck mounting grid for sensors and payload fixtures.
- Keep added payload weight balanced over the drive axis to avoid traction loss on all-terrain surfaces.
- Re-torque all fasteners after the first hour of operation with a new payload.
