# Battery & BMS

CBR-1 uses a standard, customer-procured LiPo battery with a CBI-supplied battery tray and BMS.

## Installation

1. Power off CBR-1 fully before installing or removing the battery.
2. Seat the battery in the tray so the BMS connector aligns without force.
3. Confirm the BMS reports a healthy cell balance before first use.

## Care

- Store batteries at a partial charge (per your cell manufacturer's guidance) when not in use for extended periods.
- Do not use cells outside the tray/BMS's rated voltage and capacity range.
- Replace any battery showing swelling, damage, or BMS fault codes immediately.

See the CBI Robotics Battery Assembly Manual (in CushyBots brand style) for full assembly diagrams.
