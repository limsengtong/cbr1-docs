# CBR-1 Overview

CBR-1 is CBI Robotics' academic unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) — a research and teaching platform aimed at university robotics labs and student teams. It ships with a standard LiPo battery interface (customer-procured cells, CBI-supplied battery tray and BMS) and a reparability-first mechanical design, so labs can keep it running with standard, easily replaceable components rather than proprietary parts.

## Getting Started

Use the following steps to get started quickly with your CBR-1:

1. Review the [important safety notes](/docs_robots/cbr1/user-manual/safety).
2. [Unbox CBR-1](/docs_robots/cbr1/user-manual/unboxing) and inspect for shipping damage.
3. Install the LiPo battery and confirm the BMS reads a healthy charge.
4. [Power on and check status lights](/docs_robots/cbr1/user-manual/status-lights).
5. Connect a joystick or laptop and complete your [first drive](/docs_robots/cbr1/tutorials/first-drive).
6. Set up ROS 2 on an offboard PC and continue with the [ROS 2 Quickstart](/docs_software/tutorials/ros2-quickstart).

CBR-1 is now set up and ready to go.
