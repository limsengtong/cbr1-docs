# Your First Drive

1. Complete [Getting Started](/docs_robots/cbr1/user-manual/getting-started) so CBR-1 is powered on and idle.
2. Pair the included joystick, or connect over Wi-Fi/Ethernet from an offboard PC.
3. Drive CBR-1 in a small, cleared area at low speed first to confirm steering and braking response.
4. Practice a stop using the emergency stop before attempting higher-speed or payload-loaded driving.
5. Once comfortable, proceed to the [ROS 2 Quickstart](/docs_software/tutorials/ros2-quickstart) to drive via software commands instead of the joystick.
