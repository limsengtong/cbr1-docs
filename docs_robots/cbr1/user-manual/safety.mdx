# Important Safety Information

:::danger Read before operating
CBR-1 is a motorized ground vehicle capable of causing injury or property damage if operated incorrectly. Read this section fully before unboxing or powering on the robot.
:::

- Always operate CBR-1 on a supervised, cleared area free of bystanders until operators are trained.
- Confirm the emergency stop is accessible and functional before every session.
- Do not exceed the rated payload for your test surface (see [Load Testing](/docs_robots/cbr1/integration/mechanical#load-ratings)).
- Only use the CBI-supplied battery tray and BMS with customer-procured LiPo cells — do not substitute uncontrolled battery chemistries.
- Disconnect battery power before performing any maintenance described in the [Maintenance Manual](/docs_robots/cbr1/maintenance).
- Student operators should be supervised by a lab lead or instructor familiar with this manual until independently certified.
